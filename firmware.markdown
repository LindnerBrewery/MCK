Flashing Firmware:<br/>
1. Download the corresponding firmware from above.<br/>
2. Unplug the USB cable from your computer first, then unplug the middle connection cable. Do NOT hot-plug the middle cable under power, as this may damage the controller. Flash each keyboard separately.<br/>
3. Take one keyboard, open Vial, and assign any key to "Reset" following the steps shown in images 1, 2, and 3:<br/>
![img](/static/trackball/img.png){: width="100%" }<br/>
4. After assigning the Reset key, press it. A removable drive will appear on your computer. Do not modify any files on it—simply drag the downloaded firmware file into this drive.<br/>
5. Once the firmware file copy completes, the drive will disappear automatically, indicating that flashing is complete.<br/>
6. Repeat the process for the other keyboard.<br/>
7. After both keyboards are flashed, reconnect the middle cable first, then plug in the USB cable to resume normal use.<br/>
